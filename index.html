Reflection:I learned how to make the navigation links go to the right and how to connect everything so that it is not a big mess or all bunched together. I have seen websites where the navigation links are normally on the right side and the logo is on the left side. I have also seen hero sections centered on landing pages with the headline in the middle. Responsive cards are normally seen on shopping sites and streaming sites, where the cards can move into new rows on a smaller screen. Overall, this assignment helped me understand how Flexbox works to create layouts that can be adjusted to different screen sizes.
